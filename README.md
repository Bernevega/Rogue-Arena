# Rogue Arena

**Rogue Arena** is a fast-paced roguelike shooter made in **Unity** with **C#** as part of the **Game Programming I** course at **Kajaani University of Applied Sciences**.

## About the Game

The game was developed in **2025** over the course of **2 months** by **Ann Bernevega** and **Juho Karjalainen**.

The theme of the project had to be connected to **sports**, which inspired the enemy types and overall visual style.

In Rogue Arena, the player fights through **three waves of enemies** and then faces a **final boss** that combines the abilities of all enemy types.

## Gameplay

- Survive three enemy waves with random spawns
- Fight against enemies inspired by:
  - American football
  - Basketball
  - Frisbee
- Choose one bonus after each round:
  - Increased attack speed
  - +1 heart
  - Increased movement speed
- Collect coins for extra score
- Defeat the final boss after round three

## Controls

- **W, A, S, D** – Move
- **Left Mouse Button** – Shoot
- **E** – Choose bonus
- **Esc** – Pause / Resume

## Visual Style

The game uses simple **pixel graphics**, mostly created in **16x16 resolution**, with some assets in **32x32**.  
The color palette is inspired by **sports fields**, especially **American football**.

## Technology

- **Game Engine:** Unity 6
- **Programming Language:** C#
- **Graphics:** Adobe Photoshop
